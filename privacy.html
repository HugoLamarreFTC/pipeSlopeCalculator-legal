Privacy Policy for PipeSlopeCalculator

Effective Date: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PipeSlopeCalculator ("the App") handles your data. We respect your privacy and have designed the App to operate with complete transparency.

1. Data Collection and Usage

PipeSlopeCalculator is a fully offline, standalone utility application. The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal information such as names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or crash logs. The App does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

2. Local Data Storage

The App includes a "History" feature that allows you to save your recent calculations. This data is saved entirely locally on your device using standard Apple storage frameworks.

  • This saved data never leaves your device.
  • It is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • You can permanently delete this local history at any time using the "Clear All" or swipe-to-delete functions within the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks. There are no hidden background processes monitoring your activity.

4. App Permissions

The App does not request or require any specialized iOS device permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, or contacts).

5. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new features in the future that require data collection (such as optional cloud syncing), we will update this Privacy Policy and notify you within the App before any data is collected.

6. Contact Us

PipeSlopeCalculator is developed and maintained by ForThemCreation. If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: forthemcreation@gmail.com.
