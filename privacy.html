Privacy Policy for PipeSlopeCalculator

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PipeSlopeCalculator ("the App") handles your data. We respect your privacy and have designed the App to operate with complete transparency.

1. Data Collection and Usage

PipeSlopeCalculator is a fully offline, standalone utility application. The App does not collect, transmit, or share personal information such as names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or crash logs. The App does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function.

2. Local Data Storage

The App includes a "History" feature that lets you save calculations you choose to keep. This data is saved entirely locally on your device using the standard on-device storage provided by your operating system.

  • This saved data never leaves your device.
  • It is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.
  • You can permanently delete this local history at any time from within the App — remove individual entries, or use "Clear All" to erase every saved calculation.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks. There are no hidden background processes monitoring your activity.

For the optional "Rate the App" action, the App uses the review service built into the store it was installed from — Apple's StoreKit on iOS, Google Play In-App Review on Android. These services are operated by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies. The App sends them no personal information, and receives no information back about whether you left a review or what you wrote.

4. App Permissions

The App uses your device's motion sensors solely to provide the optional slope measurement feature. Motion data is processed in real time on your device, is never recorded or stored, and is never transmitted off your device. The App does not request or require any other permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, or contacts).

5. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce new features in the future that require data collection (such as optional cloud syncing), we will update this Privacy Policy and notify you within the App before any data is collected.

6. Contact Us

PipeSlopeCalculator is developed and maintained by ForThemCreation. If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: forthemcreation@gmail.com.

7. Website Hosting

This privacy policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit this page, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and server request data as part of providing web hosting infrastructure.
